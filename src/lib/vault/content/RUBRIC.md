# Vault content authoring rubric

Use `make-money-online.ts` as the quality reference. Every niche file must match this bar.

## Counts and IDs

- Exactly 10 Quora entries and 10 Pinterest entries per niche.
- IDs are stable: `q-<prefix>-01` … `q-<prefix>-10` and `p-<prefix>-01` … `p-<prefix>-10`.
- Prefixes: `mmo`, `wl`, `hf`, `bs`, `rel`, `tg`, `pet`, `hg`.
- `nicheId` must match the file's niche exactly.

## Link placeholder

- Quora `answer` contains `__LINK__` exactly once.
- Pinterest `pinDescription` contains `__LINK__` exactly once.
- Never put `__LINK__` in titles, questions, search queries, board names, or image concepts.

## Quora answers

- At least 180 words (target 200+).
- Lead with a real, useful answer. The link is a resource, not the pitch.
- Place the link in the last third, framed as "if you want a step-by-step version" or similar.
- Avoid spam triggers: ALL CAPS, "make $10k this week", "guaranteed", "secret method", "click here".
- Write like a credible person who has done the work, not a sales page.
- Each of the 10 entries uses a distinct angle (beginner mistake, first dollar, scam filter, time budget, etc.).
- Do not reuse sentences or closing paragraphs across entries or niches.

## Pinterest pins

- `pinTitle` ≤ 100 characters. Benefit-led, not clickbait.
- `pinDescription` ≤ 500 characters, includes `__LINK__` once, plus 2–4 natural keywords.
- `boardName` is a board a beginner would actually create.
- `imageConcept` is specific enough to build in Canva (layout, text overlay, colors).
- `keywords` are 4–8 search terms people type into Pinterest.

## Voice

- Audience is a complete beginner, often 50–60, who wants affiliate income without jargon.
- Short sentences. Concrete steps. No slang walls.
- Never mention AI CashWave, 1-Tap, or internal product names in the copy members will paste.
